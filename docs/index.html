04 / THE PROJECTION7 DIMENSIONS → 2 DIMENSIONS

The cost of a simpler picture

What survives when seven become two?

Multidimensional scaling tries to keep the distances between profiles consistent while placing them on a flat surface. It preserves much of the broad structure, but changes some nearest-neighbor relationships.

ACTUAL AGGREGATE RESULTSHow much distance was preserved?

Medians of 1,225 distinct profile-pair distances, grouped into equal-count bins. The diagonal represents perfect agreement. Pairwise comparisons are not statistically independent.

READING THE MAP

Useful from afar.
Imperfect up close.

The projected map preserved the rank ordering of pairwise distances well (ρ ≈ 0.90), but only 54.4% of five-nearest-neighbor relationships remained unchanged.

Positions are a guide to similarity under one distance model, not permanent identities. A nearby point is not necessarily a close match in every dimension.

05 / THE GROUPING QUESTIONASSIGNMENT ≠ DISCOVERY

A useful negative result

Four groups are not four types.

A clustering algorithm will make groups when asked. A silhouette diagnostic tells us whether those groups are well separated. Here, the evidence for discrete clusters is weak.

FOUR-GROUP SILHOUETTE0.160

Weak separation in the seven normalized dimensions. The four named roles are an interface metaphor, not a discovered psychological taxonomy.

A COMPARISON, NOT A VERDICTHow partition choice changes the score

Ward hierarchical clustering on normalized composite traits. Higher silhouette scores indicate stronger separation; no external labels were available.

07 / RESEARCH METHODREPRODUCE · QUESTION · REASSESS

Behind the visuals

Show the work. Keep its limits.

The statistics are reproduced from the committed baseline workbook by a standalone Python analysis. The presentation does not ingest questionnaire submissions or publish respondent-level records.

01 / PREPARE

Aggregate

Construct seven descriptive composites by summing the 29 available TCI subscales. Scoring conventions require independent instrument-level verification.

02 / COMPARE

Correlate

Calculate 21 Pearson correlations and control the exploratory false discovery rate with Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment.

03 / PROJECT

Embed

Min-max scale seven composites; calculate Euclidean pairwise distances; fit two-dimensional metric MDS with a fixed random seed.

04 / TEST

Evaluate

Assess rank agreement, normalized stress, nearest-neighbor retention, and Ward-clustering silhouettes rather than interpreting shapes at face value.

Interpretation boundaries

These results describe 50 baseline records, not a representative population or a validated clinical study. The subscale-to-composite aggregation has not been independently checked against the TCI scoring manual. The statistical tests assume independent profiles. No causal, diagnostic, or population-level claim follows from the correlations. Clustering is descriptive, and the animated network scenes are illustrative rather than observed longitudinal data.
